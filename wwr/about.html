HISTORY

Founded in 2008 by a group of passionate river guides and outdoor enthusiasts, Echo River Adventures began as a small, family-run operation along the wild and scenic stretches of the Echo River. What started with just a couple of rafts and a dream to share the magic of whitewater with others has grown into one of the region’s most trusted adventure companies. With a deep respect for nature, a commitment to safety, and a love for creating unforgettable experiences, Echo River Adventures has guided thousands of thrill-seekers through rapids, canyons, and crystal-clear waters—building a legacy of adventure, connection, and environmental stewardship along the way.
